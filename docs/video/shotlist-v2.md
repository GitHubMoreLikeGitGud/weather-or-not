# Weather-or-Not demo video v2: shot list

File `weather-or-not-demo-v2.mp4` (uploaded separately, not committed): 1920x1080, 30 fps, 3:21.4 (201.4 s), H.264 + AAC 48 kHz, loudness normalised to -14 LUFS. Captions are burned in and also provided as `weather-or-not-demo-v2.srt`; the spoken text is in [`narration-v2.md`](narration-v2.md).

This is a re-cut of the first video ([`shotlist.md`](shotlist.md), [`narration.md`](narration.md)), made from the same 24 October takes plus two replays of saved answer cards and new still cards. It opens on the filing's own sentence and the answer in plain words, says what the confidence label means, shows the memo at a readable size, moves the American Electric Power restatement after the answer, adds the one contrast case (Southern Co, "partly supported") and explains the footer's tokens per second.

**LIVE shots** (label bar "LIVE") are the first video's footage: a headless-browser capture of the local stack on one PC on 24 October 2026 (the Weather-or-Not UI and agent server on a scratch copy of the data directory, the Zetaris gateway, Zetaris Lightning in Docker, Nemotron 3 Nano 4B in Ollama). The FirstEnergy question was typed and run on camera (run `r20261024T232733f392`); the run is time-lapsed at 7x with three readable 1.5x beats. That run was recorded while the headless browser took screenshots of the page on the same machine, so its planner rows and footer show fewer tokens per second (median 96) than the four clean runs recorded without capture (139.2 to 140.4); the label bar and the narration say so. The American Electric Power conflict is the saved clean run's; the override was signed and posted on camera by the local demo identity, and its reason was typed for the demo (labelled). The Zetaris shots come from the Zetaris UI of the same instance; the login is not shown and the Data Explorer tree was scrolled so that only the WON_* databases are in frame.

**REPLAY shots** (label bar "REPLAY") were rendered on 25 October 2026, without a server, a gateway or a model: the saved run files of the live FirstEnergy run (`r20261024T232733f392`) and of Southern Co's run of 25 October (`r20261025T115028c6c5`) were passed to the repository's own `renderCard` and `renderFooter` (`ui/views/card.js`, `ui/views/telemetry.js`, at commit `29d4bff`) inside `ui/index.html` with `ui/styles.css`, and a headless browser recorded the page while it scrolled. The cards carry their own "replay of a saved run" badge. Every number on them is the saved run's; the only difference from the 24 October capture of the FirstEnergy card is the confidence sentence, which now states the rule in words.

Nothing was retouched and no text was replaced. Edits in post: the cuts, the camera windows (crops and zooms of the captured pages), the speed changes (7x, 2.5x, 1.5x; each labelled in the bar) and the label bar. A drawn pointer and click ripple mark where each scripted click lands in the LIVE footage.

Still shots: the opening card (FirstEnergy's Q1 2025 10-Q sentence on heating degree days, read by hand from the filing, accession 0001031296-25-000017, as in [`docs/demo-case.md`](../demo-case.md); the agent does not read that text; then the same card with the agent's answer), the title card, the repository's architecture diagram `docs/architecture.png`, an evaluation card (numbers from `README.md` and `docs/eval-report.md`, all measured) and a closing card with the data attributions.

Data on screen: SEC EDGAR XBRL frames, companyconcept and submissions (US government work, public domain; queried live through Zetaris with a declared User-Agent, never redistributed), NOAA GHCN-Daily (CC0-1.0; a work of the US government; cite NOAA NCEI) and the company_dns SIC list (Apache-2.0 service), all read through Zetaris.

Narration: a synthetic text-to-speech voice, the same voice as the first video. No face, no human voice, no music.
