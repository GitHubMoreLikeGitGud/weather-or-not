# Weather-or-Not demo video v3: shot list

File `weather-or-not-demo-v3.mp4` (uploaded separately, not committed): 1920x1080, 30 fps, 3:13.1 (193.1 s), H.264 + AAC 48 kHz, loudness normalised to -14 LUFS. Captions are burned in and also provided as `weather-or-not-demo-v3.srt`; the spoken text is in [`narration-v3.md`](narration-v3.md).

This is the second re-cut, made from the same takes as v2 ([`shotlist-v2.md`](shotlist-v2.md), [`narration-v2.md`](narration-v2.md)) after the round-2 review. What changed from v2:

- The Ask shot starts just after Ask is clicked. The form still shows FirstEnergy, CY2025Q1 and the claim, and the timeline already shows the new live run; v2's frames whose timeline still showed the previously opened saved run (American Electric Power) are cut.
- The silent time-lapses of the run play at 10x (v2: 7x); the three narrated beats stay at 1.5x.
- The evidence drawer carries a note on its first line, "56 of 56 stations": the one Lightning SQL statement computes degree days for all 56 seed stations (FirstEnergy's 9 and its peers'), and the card's figures use FirstEnergy's 9.
- The Zetaris Data Quality panel is held at 1x with a note: in that panel the coverage_ok row's record columns read "-", and the note gives the rule's onboarding run through Lightning SQL (`RUN DQ coverage_ok`, 24 October 2026): 342 rows checked, 342 valid, 0 invalid, as recorded in `README.md` ("The DQ gate") and `docs/eval-report.md` section 5. A new spoken line says the same.
- The footer line now says what the footer counts (eight planner calls, one repaired, no fallbacks, zero cloud calls). The footer's tokens per second were recorded under screen capture; the label bar says so ("tok/s slowed by screen capture"), and the clean runs' figures are in `README.md` and `docs/nemotron-bench.md`.
- The two answer cards were re-rendered by the UI at commit `d2f207d`, so their range labels match the README: the weather and industry ranges are the 10th to 90th percentile of the peer resamples, and the remainder range combines their bounds.

**LIVE shots** (label bar "LIVE") are the first video's footage: a headless-browser capture of the local stack on one PC on 24 October 2026 (the Weather-or-Not UI and agent server on a scratch copy of the data directory, the Zetaris gateway, Zetaris Lightning in Docker, Nemotron 3 Nano 4B in Ollama). The FirstEnergy question was typed and run on camera (run `r20261024T232733f392`); the run is time-lapsed at 10x with three readable 1.5x beats. That run was recorded while the headless browser took screenshots of the page on the same machine, so its planner rows and footer show fewer tokens per second (median 96) than the four clean runs recorded without capture (139.2 to 140.4); the label bar says so. The American Electric Power conflict is the saved clean run's; the override was signed and posted on camera by the local demo identity, and its reason was typed for the demo (labelled). The Zetaris shots come from the Zetaris UI of the same instance; the login is not shown and the Data Explorer tree was scrolled so that only the WON_* databases are in frame.

**REPLAY shots** (label bar "REPLAY") were rendered on 25 October 2026, without a server, a gateway or a model: the saved run files of the live FirstEnergy run (`r20261024T232733f392`) and of Southern Co's run of 25 October (`r20261025T115028c6c5`) were passed to the repository's own `renderCard` and `renderFooter` (`ui/views/card.js`, `ui/views/telemetry.js`, at commit `d2f207d`) inside `ui/index.html` with `ui/styles.css`, and a headless browser recorded the page while it scrolled. The cards carry their own "replay of a saved run" badge. Every number on them is the saved run's; the differences from the 24 October capture of the FirstEnergy card are wording only: the confidence sentence states the rule in words, the range labels say how each range is built, and the claim panel says the rule has no upper bound.

Nothing was retouched and no text was replaced. Edits in post: the cuts, the camera windows (crops and zooms of the captured pages), the speed changes (10x, 2.5x, 1.5x; each labelled in the bar), the label bar, and two notes added in the edit (a dark box with an amber border: the drawer's "56 of 56 stations" and the coverage rule's onboarding run, with an outline around the panel cells it explains). A drawn pointer and click ripple mark where each scripted click lands in the LIVE footage.

Still shots: the opening card (FirstEnergy's Q1 2025 10-Q sentence on heating degree days, read by hand from the filing, accession 0001031296-25-000017, as in [`docs/demo-case.md`](../demo-case.md); the agent does not read that text; then the same card with the agent's answer), the title card, the repository's architecture diagram `docs/architecture.png`, an evaluation card (numbers from `README.md`, `docs/eval-report.md` and `docs/nemotron-bench.md`, all measured) and a closing card with the data attributions.

Data on screen: SEC EDGAR XBRL frames, companyconcept and submissions (US government work, public domain; queried live through Zetaris with a declared User-Agent, never redistributed), NOAA GHCN-Daily (CC0-1.0; a work of the US government; cite NOAA NCEI) and the company_dns SIC list (Apache-2.0 service), all read through Zetaris.

Narration: a synthetic text-to-speech voice, the same voice as the first video. No face, no human voice, no music.
